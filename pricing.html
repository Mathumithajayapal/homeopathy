Pricing FAQs

Common questions about our billing, plans, and insurance.

Do you accept health insurance?

Currently, homeopathy is generally not covered by major health insurance providers. However, we can provide a detailed receipt for Health Savings Accounts (HSA) or Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) if eligible.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. Many patients start with a single session and choose to upgrade to a Chronic Care or Family plan. We will prorate the cost of your initial session towards the new plan.

Are the homeopathic medicines included?

Standard remedies are included in our Chronic and Family plans. For single visits, remedies are billed separately (usually between $15 - $30 depending on the prescription).

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. Late cancellations may be subject to a $40 fee to accommodate other patients.
