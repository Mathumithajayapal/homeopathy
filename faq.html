General & Treatments

Is homeopathy safe for children and pregnant women?

Yes, homeopathic remedies are highly diluted and completely non-toxic. They are perfectly safe for infants, children, pregnant women, and the elderly with no risk of overdose.

How long does it take for treatments to work?

It depends on the condition. Acute conditions like colds may respond within hours or days. Chronic conditions that have existed for years may take a few months to fully resolve.

Can I take homeopathic medicine with my regular prescriptions?

Absolutely. Homeopathy does not interfere or negatively interact with conventional drugs. However, always inform your homeopath about any medications you are currently taking.

What are the remedies made of?

Remedies are derived from natural sources, predominantly plants and minerals. They are prepared through a specific process of sequential dilution and succussion (vigorous shaking).

Do you treat chronic psychological issues?

Yes. Homeopathy treats the individual as a whole. We routinely help patients manage anxiety, depression, insomnia, and stress by addressing the underlying imbalances.

Are there dietary restrictions while on homeopathic treatment?

Generally, we advise avoiding very strong flavors like raw garlic, raw onion, coffee, and camphor just 15 minutes before and after taking a remedy, as strong odors can neutralize it.

Booking & Payments

Does health insurance cover homeopathic treatment?

Coverage depends entirely on your specific insurance provider and policy. Some holistic health add-ons cover our services. We provide detailed receipts for you to submit claims.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations. Appointments canceled with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a 50% late cancellation fee.

How much does an initial consultation cost?

Initial consultations run between 45-60 minutes and cost $80 for an in-clinic visit or $60 for a video consultation. Remedies are billed separately.

Do you offer payment plans for chronic care?

Yes, we offer discounted packages for long-term chronic treatments (e.g., 3-month or 6-month plans) which can be paid in monthly installments.

How do I access my online video consultation?

Once your booking is confirmed, you will receive an email with a secure Zoom link. Simply click the link 5 minutes before your scheduled appointment time.

Can I request a specific doctor?

Yes, you can choose your preferred practitioner during the online booking process, depending on their individual availability schedules.

How to Prepare for Your Visit

Three simple steps to ensure you get the most out of your first consultation.

1. Gather Records

Bring any recent blood tests, medical reports, and a complete list of conventional medications you are currently taking.

2. Note Symptoms

Homeopathy relies on exact details. Note down what triggers your symptoms, what makes them better or worse, and time of day they occur.

3. Avoid Strong Odors

Please refrain from drinking coffee, brushing your teeth with mint toothpaste, or wearing strong perfumes 30 minutes prior to your visit.
