Before You Reach Out

Quick answers to common questions about visiting our clinic.

Do you accept walk-in patients?

To ensure every patient gets the time they deserve, we operate strictly by appointment. However, we do keep limited slots open for acute cases. Please call ahead to check availability.

Can I do my initial consultation online?

Yes! We offer comprehensive video consultations. While in-person visits are great, our digital setup allows our experts to accurately assess and prescribe remedies from afar.

Are remedies included in the consult fee?

Initial consultation fees cover the extensive diagnostic process. Specially compounded homeopathic remedies are dispensed separately by our pharmacy at a nominal cost.
